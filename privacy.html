SAVORA Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: MAY 2026

Welcome to SAVORA. We are committed to protecting your privacy and securing the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information within our loyalty network.

1. Data We Collect

We practice strict data minimization and only collect the essential, non-sensitive information required to provide our services:

  • Identity Information: Your chosen username/display name.
  • Contact Information: Your email address.
  • System Identifiers: A unique alphanumeric Card Identification Number (Card ID) assigned to your account tier.
  • Operational Metadata: Timestamped location validation data and IP addresses captured securely when your card is scanned or tapped at a partner merchant address.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

We process your data strictly for the following operational needs:

  • Service Delivery: To verify your student status, manage account registration, and apply correct loyalty limits based on your access tier (e.g., Vault vs. Lite).
  • Security & Anti-Fraud: To authenticate transactions, protect system integrity, and ensure subscription tiers are non-transferable.
  • Gamification & Analytics: To run our platform leaderboard systems and compile completely anonymized, aggregated analytical reports for our merchant partners (via our secure B L Λ C K B O X™ backend infrastructure). We never share your raw, identifying personal details with external vendors.
  • Communications: To deliver automated transaction logs, digital receipts, and platform updates directly to your email.

3. Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your data based on your explicit Consent provided during account setup, and out of Contractual Necessity to deliver the exclusive partner discounts and tier features you sign up for.

4. Data Retention & Erasure

Your data is kept secure for as long as your account is active. To maintain platform efficiency, temporary data logs used for campus leaderboard metrics are automatically cleared or reset at regular operational intervals.

5. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA), you hold full control over your digital identity. You have the right to:

  • Access and request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Rectify or correct any inaccurate personal details (such as updating your linked card).
  • Request the absolute deletion of your data (Right to be Forgotten).

6. Security Controls

Your data travels via secure HTTPS encryption (SSL) protocols. Administrative access to our internal databanks is guarded by multi-factor authentication and strict access logs to prevent unauthorized data exposure.

7. Contact Us

To exercise your data rights, request profile modification, or lodge an inquiry, contact our support desk at: savoradigitals@gmail.com
