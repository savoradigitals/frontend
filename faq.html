Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Savora and BLACKBOX™

What is Savora?

Savora is a student loyalty platform that connects verified students with exclusive discounts and rewards from trusted businesses across Nigeria.

Who can access BLACKBOX™?

Sign up as a verified student, explore available deals, visit participating businesses, and enjoy exclusive student offers while earning rewards.

How do I become a partner?

Submit your application through the contact channels on Savora. Applications are reviewed before credentials are issued.

I forgot my partner credentials.

Contact Savora support for identity verification and credential recovery.

How secure is my information?

All sensitive communications are encrypted and protected through multiple security layers.

How long does approval take?

Most applications are reviewed within 24–72 hours.

How can I contact support?

Email: support@savora.ng
